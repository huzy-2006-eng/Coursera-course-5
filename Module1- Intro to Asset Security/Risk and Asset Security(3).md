# NIST Cybersecurity Framework - (CSF)
A voluntary framework that consists of standards, guidelines and best practices to manage cybersecurity risk.

## Origins of the framework -
Originally released in 2014, NIST developed the Cybersecurity Framework to protect critical infrastucture in United States.
The goal was to make the framework more flexible, making it easier to adopt for small  businesses or anyone else that might lack the resources to develop their own security plans.


## Three NIST CSF components- 
1. #### Core - 
Five functions of the NIST CSF Core:
1. Identify - most important assets.
2. Protect - those assets with appropriate safeguards.
3. Detect - Detect Attacks.
4. Respond - develop response.
5. Recover - develop recovery plans.
6. Govern - emphasize the importance of leadership and decision-making when it comes to managing cybersecurity risks.

2. #### Tiers - 
The CSF tiers are a way of measuring the sophistication of an organization's cybersecurity program.
CSF tiers are measured on a scale of 1 to 4. Tier 1 is the lowest score, indicating that a limited set of security controls have been implemented.
Overall, CSF tiers are used to assess an organization's security posture and identify areas for improvement.

3. #### Profiles - 
Thee CSF profiles are pre-made templates of the NIST CSF that are developed by a team of industry experts.
CSF profiles are tailored to address the specific risks of an organization or industry.
They are used to help organizations develop a baseline for their security plans, or as a way of comparing their current cybersecurity posture to a specific industry standard.
